Harbor Notes Reading Club Guidelines

1. Welcome. The Harbor Notes club meets every Thursday evening at the town library.
2. Books. Members vote on the next book at the start of each month.
3. Seating. Chairs are arranged in a circle so that everyone can see each other.
4. Snacks. Members take turns bringing tea and biscuits to the meeting.
5. Respect. Please listen carefully and let others finish their sentences.
6. Notes. A volunteer writes short notes and shares them by email afterwards.
7. Visitors. Friends are welcome to join a meeting before becoming members.
8. Quiet hours. The library asks everyone to keep voices low after eight o'clock.
