Harbor Notes Reading Club Guidelines (friendly edition)

1. Welcome. The Harbor Notes club happily meets every single Thursday evening at the town library.
2. Books. Members vote together on the next book at the start of each new month.
3. Seating. Chairs are gently arranged in a circle so that everyone can easily see each other.
4. Snacks. Members take turns bringing warm tea and biscuits to the weekly meeting.
5. Respect. Please listen very carefully and let others calmly finish their sentences.
6. Notes. A kind volunteer writes short notes and shares them by email soon afterwards.
7. Visitors. Friends are always welcome to join a meeting before becoming full members.
8. Quiet hours. The library politely asks everyone to keep voices low after eight o'clock.
